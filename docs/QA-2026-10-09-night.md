# QA session 2026-10-09 (night), gate build, real-input playtest

Build: target\gate\release\space-peace.exe, `--volume 0`, scratch APPDATA (`%TEMP%\space-peace-qa\appdata`).
Screenshots: `%TEMP%\space-peace-qa\shots\` (numbers 001..154 referenced below).
Session was about 9 minutes of play, not 30-40. It was cut short once the battle problem below blocked further battle testing. Coverage is listed at the end.
Performance: 124-149 FPS at 2400x1350 throughout, galaxy, planet view and battle.

## Findings

### P0 / P1

**P1-1 Space battle shows a planet-surface terrain, ships not visible (blocks meaningful battle play)** - shots 142, 144, 145, 147, 149
- Did: fleet (Gladiator SD plus a second ship) to Brentaal, banner, BEGIN, loading screen, battle.
- Expected (EaW): a space map with the nebula/skybox, the player's ships framed by the start camera, and the enemy fleet and station.
- Happened: the whole screen is a huge brown mountainous ground mesh. The flagship is only a tiny shape at the bottom edge in shot 142. After mouse-wheel zoom and minimap clicks the view shows only terrain and no ships. The minimap is a flat brown square with blob shapes. I could not select ships, issue move/attack orders, reach hardpoints, abilities or reinforcements, or win a battle.
- Note: the log said "reinforcements: 0 reserve unit types", and the banner shown on the second battle names a Venator on the enemy side. The first banner had no enemy flagship at all (`""`).
- Possible cause (not verified): the planet-terrain/ground map is being used as the space backdrop, or the camera starts far from the ships. The wheel direction in battle might also be inverted relative to the galaxy.
- Severity: P1, P0 if reproducible for every space battle.

**P1-2 Retreat from the battle flipped Brentaal to the Empire** - shots 151, 154
- Did: Esc, then RETREAT (battle abandoned at t=94 s, units lost [0,0]).
- Expected: the fleet withdraws and the planet stays enemy-owned.
- Happened: Brentaal, which was red, is green in the galaxy view afterwards, with green unit pips, as if conquered. Just before the Save-game click a second battle, "battle 2 pending at Brentaal", appeared with a Venator defender. So the state is inconsistent: the first banner showed no enemy ships, then the planet flipped, then a new battle with enemy ships.
- Severity: P1.

**P1-3 Save slot button caught a click meant for the dialog; no confirmation of what was saved** - shots 153-154
- Save worked (`saves\slot3.json`, 220 KB), but slot labels stay "EMPTY" until reopened and there is no "saved" feedback. Minor. Load was not tested.
- Severity: P2.

### P2 / polish

**P2-1 Save dialog layout overlaps the Pause dialog** - shot 153
- The "SAVE GAME" panel is shorter than the 5 slots plus BACK. BACK overhangs the panel bottom and the Pause dialog's "PAUSED" title and QUIT button show through behind it. Expected: one clean panel.

**P2-2 Battle Esc menu has only RESUME / RETREAT** - shot 148
- There is no Save/Load/Options in battle. This may be intended (EaW's battle menu has Resume, Retreat and options), so I have not rated it. The galaxy Esc menu does have Resume, Save, Load, Options, Main Menu and Quit (shot 152).

**P2-3 Commander/selection panel is empty after a click in the galaxy** - shots 120, 136
- The top-left info panel title and credit value are blank after a fleet is deselected (shot 136: header row with no name). Expected: the last selection or the capital.

**P2-4 Log WARNs at start** - 3 trade routes skipped: "unresolved end point" (WW_064, WW_126, WW_127: Allanteen to Loronar/Atzerri/Mandalore). Data issue, harmless in play.

**P2-5 Log flood during battle loading** - "longest frame while loading 48 ms" is logged about every 7 ms, thousands of lines.

## What worked

- Main menu, campaign select and galaxy load. Galaxy renders with labels, route lines, nebulae and the minimap.
- Wheel zoom in/out across the whole range, down to the planet view (planet, orbital station, "BASE LAYOUT" ground view and info text).
- Left-click select planet or fleet; the hover/info panel shows the full EaW text.
- Build: a ship (750-credit item) was queued with a build-progress thumbnail. Structure tooltips show name, cost and build time. An Outpost was queued from the ground-structures tab, and the tooltip also shows requirements.
- Right-click move: the yellow route line was drawn, the fleet moved, and it arrived at Brentaal after about 6 s.
- Battle banner ("SPACE BATTLE IMMINENT", BEGIN / AUTO-RESOLVE), loading screen with BEGIN, retreat countdown (0:55 down to 0:08 seen on the retreat button), Esc menu and RESUME.
- Save game to slot works.
