## Crash / blocker

None crashed. C1 below blocks playing a space battle comfortably, so it is listed first.

### C1. Space battle opens in a top-down view of a giant flat planet surface, no space visible
- Repro: Remake 4.0, From the Ground Up, Underworld, Brutal. Build 5 ships at Ryloth (items 320, 600 x2, 2500, 4000), select the fleet pad,
  right-click Tatooine (Independent, Pirate Asteroid Base there). Dialog "SPACE BATTLE IMMINENT", click BEGIN, briefing screen, click BEGIN.
- Expected: black space with the planet as a backdrop, ships as models (reference: HANDOFF "opening camera" B1).
- Actual: the camera looks straight down and the desert planet texture fills the whole screen. Ships are only 2D strategic icons.
  A hard vertical terrain seam is visible and a large blob-shaped shadow of the fleet lies on the ground. Mouse wheel zoom out (18 notches)
  does not reveal space. End, Home and Page Up barely change or leave an empty view.
- Log at start: `battle camera: fleet centre (-6198,-41,1989), median spread 2248, start distance 1300`;
  `battle ground below the plane: top at -4974 / 5459 / 11910 (plane 0), position y -5989 / -10000 / 5822`.
- What does work: pressing C (cinematic) switched to a side view with black space, the atmosphere rim and the planet below, which is
  the expected look (shot `002-b-cine`, `001-b-late`). So the default camera pitch and distance after BEGIN is the bug.
- Shots: `001-battle2`, `001-b-zout2`, `001-b-box`, `002-b-attack` (bad); `002-b-cine`, `002-b-saved` (good).

## Broken
