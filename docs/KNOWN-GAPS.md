# Known gaps

What is missing or unverified, one line each. Every line says what is missing, how to observe it and where it lives.
"(unverified)" marks a line that could not be confirmed against the code or a running game. Out of scope on purpose:
ground combat, story campaigns, cutscenes, non-English text and speech. Rules that are estimates rather than game
data are labelled in [RULES.md](RULES.md).

## Galactic Conquest

- **Fleets that reach a planet within seconds of each other fight separate battles.** The first arrival opens and resolves a battle, so a force split over several lanes loses piecemeal; there is no gather window and late arrivals do not join. Observe: send three fleets from different planets to one defended planet so they arrive about 1 s apart, or run `ai_check` with `ATTACKS=1`. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim` (`detect_battles`), `crates/galaxy-play`. (unverified)
- **Heroes vanish in "From the Ground Up".** Heroes start with the fleets outside the kept start planet and are removed with them, so an Underworld start shows no hero portrait. Observe: new game, From the Ground Up, Underworld; the top-right portrait bar is empty. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/ground_up.rs`. (unverified)
- **Some spy flags have no place in the interface.** `See_Credit_Income_Breakdown`, `See_Most_Powerful_Ship`, `See_Political_Control(_Breakdown)`, `See_Num_Ground_Companies` and `See_Ground_Company_Contents` are read but nothing shows them (no ground forces, no political control, no income breakdown card); the spied lines appear on the galaxy hover card only, not in the planet close-up. Observe: put `MTC_Spy_R` next to an Empire planet and hover it. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/agents.rs` (`SpyFlags`), `crates/eaw-remake/src/play_ui.rs` (`spy_lines`).
- **Corruption, black market income, slice abilities and trade route income are not modelled.** The agents' credit siphon is (RULES 1.14). Observe: hero abilities that name `Planet_Black_Market_Modifier` have no effect on income. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim` (`World::planet_income`).
- **The galaxy camera start distance and tilt come from the galaxy extent, not from `GMC_InitialPullbackDistance` (470) and `GMC_ZoomedPitchAngleDegrees` (-20).** Observe: start any campaign, zoom to a planet and compare with a Remake frame. Where: `crates/eaw-remake/src/camera.rs`.
- **Three galaxy look details differ from the Remake:** the build bar is a bright maroon strip with a bright top edge (the Remake's is dark and flat), a maroon haze sits at the galaxy centre, and a dense speckle layer in the sky reads as noise. Observe: start `GC_Full_Web_Way_Rebel`, look at the bottom bar and the map centre, or `--offscreen out.png --screen galaxy`. Where: `crates/eaw-remake/src/galaxy_map.rs`, `crates/eaw-ui`. (unverified)
- **Planet view lacks the moon behind the middle fleet box that the Remake shows at Yavin.** Observe: zoom into Yavin with the wheel. Where: `crates/eaw-remake/src/planet_view.rs`. (unverified)
- **The hero badge in the planet-view fleet boxes is the green medal `i_icon_hero_you.tga`, a stand-in for the Remake's blue circle with a figure.** Observe: grant `Mon_Mothma` at Yavin and open the planet view. Where: `crates/eaw-remake/src/planet_view.rs`.
- **Hidden versus greyed build items follow our rule, not a verified engine rule.** Tech above the level, story locks, planet restrictions and star-base-capped items are hidden; ships with a missing shipyard or star base level are greyed. The game strings `TEXT_REQUIRED_PLANET_RESTRICTION` and `TEXT_REQUIRED_TECH_LEVEL` suggest the engine greys some of the hidden ones. Observe: open the build bar on a planet that cannot build a restricted ship. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/production.rs`, `crates/galaxy-play/src/build_menu.rs`.
- **Planet capture has no landing step.** A hostile faction alone in orbit for `capture_delay_seconds` with no enemy star base flips the owner (inferred from the Remake AI scripts, not verified against the engine); ground combat, ground base levels, siege and planet shields are out of scope. Observe: park a fleet at an undefended planet. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/sim.rs` (`capture`).
- **Underworld agent mechanics beyond the siphon are not modelled.** Corruption, bribery, neutralise and black market are missing; the smuggler, underboss and field agent work (RULES 1.14). Observe: the other abilities exist only on ground or `Neutral` hero objects in the data. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/agents.rs`.
- **Agents cannot be caught.** `AntiStealthEffectiveness` in the AI equations has nothing to read, and the 4.0 counters (`Bounty_Hunter_Team_E` / `_R`, `ISB_Agent_Team`) are neutral dummies. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/agents.rs`. Not a gap: the AI never recruits or uses agents, as in the Remake, whose galactic AI goal sets (`AI/Players/GC_*Ai.xml`) contain no smuggler or siphon goal; the `Suitable_Smuggler_Target` and `Needs_Smuggler_Removed` equations exist but no goal uses them.
- **The Rebel field agent is never offered in the Remake data.** It needs the `Spy_Center`, whose `Affiliation` is `<!--Rebel-->,Neutral`. Observe: Rebel build bar at any planet. Where: data (`Buildings/Ground/GC/Rebel/Spy_Center.xml`).
- **Vanilla Underworld corruption and bribery mechanics have no active carrier in 4.0 data.** Corruption in 4.0 sets `Benefits_From_Corruption` No for all factions, the saboteur is `Neutral`, and bribery is on ground Tyber Zann; Underworld space heroes have only income, gambling and production bonuses. Observe: check Underworld planets and heroes; no corruption spread or bribery options exist in the data. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/catalog.rs`, `world.rs`.
- **Space heroes in 4.0 have no activated galactic abilities.** In 4.0's data (`Units/Space/*Hero*`), space heroes do not possess Remove Corruption (Mon Mothma has discount, income, and gambling), Neutralize Hero (Boba Fett, Bossk, IG-88, Mara Jade carry stealth and spy abilities), or Siphon Credits (Han Solo has stealth and spy); the vanilla activated abilities exist only on unused ground/neutral objects. Observe: inspect hero unit entries; abilities are passive modifiers, stealth, spy, or tactical. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/catalog.rs`, `world.rs`.
- **Galactic structure `Combat_Bonus_Ability` and `Defense_Bonus_Percentage` are not applied.** Fleet combat auras apply in auto-resolve and battle-sim, but ground power generators and Rebel asteroid bases give no bonus in auto-resolve; space bases carry only the fighter bonus. Observe: ground structures or asteroid bases in orbit. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/combat.rs`.
- **Star base stealth (`Planet_Starbase_Stealth`) and station-specific unit unlocks beyond `Required_Star_Base_Level` are not modelled.** Observe: no hidden stations on the galaxy map. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/sight.rs`, `catalog.rs`.
- **Random `INTERVENTION_*` events (pirate ambush, smuggler, revolt, refugee escort) are not triggered.** Observe: the Web Way plot list never loads them, so none occurs in a campaign. Where: `crates/galaxy-script/src/lib.rs` (story plot list).
- **Objects with `Tech_Level` 90 or higher are never offered.** Observe: `Planet_Shield_E`, `Communications_Array_E`, `Imperial_Command_Center` do not appear in any build menu (story-unlocked in the data). Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/catalog.rs`, `production.rs`.
- **`Required_Ground_Base_Level` is checked for units only.** The ground base level is the number of ground structures (RULES 1.14); the one structure with the tag has `Tech_Level` 99. Observe: no buildable structure carries it. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/production.rs`.
- **Structure upgrade items do not spawn or remove `*_ORBITAL_DUMMY` units.** The script despawns `{previous}_ORBITAL_DUMMY` when that type exists; those objects have no space model and are not in the unit catalog, so nothing is removed. Observe: upgrading a Golan leaves the fleet list unchanged. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/structure_upgrades.rs`.
- **A missing planet name in `Required_Planets` is assumed to be ignored (any one listed planet suffices).** Observe: items with a list containing an unknown planet name are buildable at the known ones. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/requirements.rs`.
- **Persistent battle damage covers only ship and star base hull and shield.** Observe: hardpoint damage, squadron fighters, structures and statistical auto-resolve results are repaired or not carried over. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/tactical.rs`, `state.rs`.
- **Repair rates, week length, capture delay and several other galactic rules are estimates.** Observe: `SimConfig` fields named in docs/RULES.md; no data source. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/state.rs`.
- **`PlayAction::ResolveBattle` always auto-resolves.** Observe: only the "Fight" path plays a tactical battle. Where: `crates/galaxy-play/src/logic.rs`, `driver.rs`.
- **Fleets in hyperspace cannot be redirected (order a retreat first).** Observe: right-click a destination while a fleet is in a lane; a notification says so. Where: `crates/galaxy-play/src/logic.rs`.
- **Long soak runs showed the AI hoarding credits (Empire 2.4M at week 100 with population far below the cap), the classic AI snowballing, and AI-vs-AI tactical resolution costing about 0.7 s per battle.** Observe: `cargo run --release -p galaxy-play --example soak -- --campaign GC_Full_Web_Way_Imperial --human Empire --seed 2 --weeks 120 --tier brutal`. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/ai.rs`, `crates/galaxy-ai` (unverified: may be fixed since the soak runs).
- **Script object ids are `(fleet, unit type)` pairs, not stable `ShipId`s.** Observe: ids held by a long-running script go stale when fleets merge or split. Where: `crates/galaxy-script/src/host.rs` (`ship_id`).
- **Ground units, hero teams and markers are not modelled.** Observe: spawn requests for them are counted in `HostStats::spawn_ignored` and dropped. Where: `crates/galaxy-script/src/host.rs`.
- **`GalacticFreeStore` is experimental and stops at the first access to the engine-provided `FreeStore` object.** Observe: `ScriptedConfig::freestore = true`. Where: `crates/galaxy-script/src/lib.rs`.
- **Snapshot saves do not capture Lua coroutine state.** Where scripts sleep and their locals, AI plans in flight, their task forces, undelivered events and frozen tactical set-ups are lost, so a loaded game continues but is not bit-identical. Observe: the save tests print whether a loaded run matches the original (it does not). Where: `crates/galaxy-script/src/save.rs`.

## Galactic AI

- **Standing fleets of the Remake factions are squadron swarms with few capital ships.** Empire holds about 3% capitals at week 80 and Rebel none, because the standing-fleet plan is data-gated, EaW has no scrapping and the population cap fills by week 40. Observe: `ai_check 80 GC_Full_Web_Way_Rebel none normal` (class mix lines). Where: `crates/galaxy-ai/src/taskforce.rs`, `crates/galaxy-script/examples/ai_check.rs`.
- **The AI never buys heroes.** The 4.0 AI budget template gives the `Hero` category 0% for every faction, and the only hero goals are `Remove_Corruption` and the Death Star. Observe: `ai_check ... none normal` with `HEROES=1`; no AI faction owns a hero ship. Where: `crates/galaxy-ai`, `crates/galaxy-script/examples/ai_check.rs`.
- **Fleets idle for 20 weeks or more.** About 10% of Empire fleet-weeks (Warlords 16%, Cartels 9%); the worst single fleet idled 55 to 69 weeks. Observe: the idle fleet-weeks column of `ai_check`. Where: `crates/galaxy-ai/src/engine.rs`.
- **Minor factions hoard credits and stand still when the human is passive** (Mandalorians, Hapan, CSA, Sith: 2 to 5 planets, 124k to 212k credits at week 80). The data gives minor AIs no expansion goals. Observe: `ai_check 80 GC_Full_Web_Way_Rebel none normal`. Where: `crates/galaxy-script`.
- **Pirate planets are walls for the Rebel AI.** The start spawn (`Spawn_Starting_Forces_Web_Way.lua`) gives each Pirate planet 1000 to 1500 combat power, 4 times a main faction's planet; the weekly rearm adds up to 2 ships per player tech level. Observe: watch Rebel attacks on Pirate planets with a Lv2 star base from week 20. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/ground_up.rs` (`rearm_passive`).
- **The AI Republic stalls against the AI CIS in `GC_Full_Web_Way_CIS` when combat auras apply in auto-resolve.** It builds 356 to 448 ships in 20 weeks but holds its 8 planets (8 -> 8, 7, 13 over seeds 1-3; it expands in 1 of seeds 1-6, without auras in 2 of seeds 1-3). Observe: `cargo test -p galaxy-sim --test clone_wars republic_and_cis_build -- --nocapture`. Where: `crates/galaxy-ai`, `crates/galaxy-sim/src/combat.rs`.
- **Hard, Brutal, the Empire as the human and the Clone Wars stack were measured with one seed only.** Observe: `ai_check <weeks> <campaign> <faction> hard`. Where: `crates/galaxy-script/examples/ai_check.rs`. (unverified)
- **Brutal's 4x credits and 0.8x build time cannot be read in the UI,** so the tier cannot be confirmed in play. Observe: start with Brutal and watch the AI treasury. Where: `crates/galaxy-script` (`BRUTAL_*`). (unverified)
- **`NBT*` perception variables are approximated by their plain versions, and unknown variables read as 0.** Observe: `EvalStats` counts unknown variables; run the `ai_vocab` example. Where: `crates/galaxy-ai/src/perception.rs`.
- **No systematic behaviour comparison with the original AI.** Observe: only internal measures exist (`ai_match`, `ai_audit`). Where: `crates/galaxy-script/tests/ai_match.rs`.
- **Ground forces are assembled instantly; only space behaviour is real.** Observe: conquer plans that need `Regiment`s never wait for them. Where: `crates/galaxy-ai/src/taskforce.rs`.
- **The AI floats very large credit reserves late in a long game.** Observe: an all-AI run of 100 weeks; spending is gated by the Remake's own desire equations (one plan builds 1 to 3 ships). Where: `crates/galaxy-ai/src/engine.rs`.
- **The pincer only triggers when one force would need 50 to 90 percent of the player's strength, and fog applies to the strategy layer, not to the original equations.** Observe: `crates/galaxy-ai/tests/strategy.rs`. Where: `crates/galaxy-ai/src/strategy.rs`.
- **AI tech research is a project heuristic, not the Remake's.** The Remake has no galactic research plan; with no human player the engine queues levels on a timer (estimate). Observe: all-AI game; `AiStats::tech_upgrades`. Where: `crates/galaxy-ai/src/engine.rs`.
- **The galaxy AI does not buy structure level upgrades or downgrades.** The built-in planner and the scripted Remake AI only queue `UnitRole::Ship`, so a Golan I never becomes a Golan II on its own. Observe: an AI planet that has a Golan I still has it after many weeks. Where: `crates/galaxy-sim/src/ai.rs`, `crates/galaxy-script/src/ai.rs` (`buildable_units`).

## Rendering

- **Shaders are approximations: no reflection maps, stencil shadow volumes, sun glare or lens flare; nebula and emissive-only materials render as additive/flat.** Observe: `render_scene` on a nebula or emissive model and compare with the game. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/alamo.wgsl`, `material.rs`.
- **Particle scale and semantics differ from the original in places.** Observe: `showcase death`; fire and smoke of a Star Destroyer death span about 1.5x the hull length at 3 s. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/particles.rs`, `battle-view` explosion cadence.
- **Looping emitters other than missile smoke (beam fronts, hardpoint glows, shockwave rings) still read `inBursts` / `isNotBillboarded` the old way.** Only engine exhausts and shield impacts draw their not-billboarded sprites flat; hull impacts and explosions still face the camera. Observe: compare those effects with the video. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/particles.rs`.
- **Shield impacts are put on boxes round the model's visible meshes, not on its collision mesh (estimate).** The simulation stops shots on a round hit sphere; the absorb effect is moved along the line to the unit's centre onto the first mesh box it meets, so on rounded hulls it can sit a little off the hull at the box corners. Observe: `battle_demo --station Rebel_Star_Base_3` with `STATION_ONLY=1 SP_DEMO_HALF=3000 SP_DEMO_FOCUS=star_base`, close camera, about 11 s. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/model.rs` (`ModelBox`).
- **Additive particle blend assumes SRCALPHA/ONE and glow-texture sampler address modes are assumed repeat.** Observe: engine glow cones at their tips. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/particle.wgsl`.
- **`EngineThrottle` is not driven by any game code; every engine runs at full intensity.** Observe: stop a ship in a battle; the exhaust does not shrink. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/particles.rs`, `crates/battle-view/src/ships.rs`.
- **No animation blending, light emitters (type 4) are not used as lights, hardpoints without models are bare bones.** Observe: switch animations on a model. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/animation.rs`, `model.rs`.
- **Skinned meshes are not batched across instances.** Observe: many animated models at 4K. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/model.rs`.
- **Custom-render projectiles are undrawn.** Observe: fire a projectile whose type names a custom render (unverified). Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/projectiles.rs`.
- **Black-hole TGA type 78 textures fail to decode.** Observe: a map that uses them. Where: `crates/alamo-model/src/image.rs` (unverified).
- **The fill lights from the map's `.ted` (lights 2 and 3) were never compared with the real game look.** Observe: render the same map in both. Where: `crates/alamo-render/src/lighting.rs`, `alamo.wgsl::fill_light`.

## Audio

- **Loudness balance was never judged against the Remake by ear.** Weapon shots reach the mixer at gains of 0.02 to 0.07 while speech sits at 0.9 and music at 0.6; the data defines no bus volumes (`Audio.xml` has none), so the falloff curve `(saturation/distance)^rolloff` and the bus defaults are estimates. Observe: a large fight with sound on. Where: `crates/alamo-audio` (`plugin.rs`, constants).
- **What `Max_Instances` does at the limit is not stated in the data.** The rule is that the quietest instance at the listener goes and a request more than 3 dB quieter is dropped; the other plausible rule ignores the new sound. Observe: `SP_AUDIO_MAXINST=drop` or `oldest` for an A/B listen in a dense fight. Where: `crates/alamo-audio/src/plugin.rs`.
- **Several voice and loop hooks are unwired:** `Reinforcements_Requesting_SFXEvent`, hero respawned lines, and the laser-defence zap and `Force_Healing_Ability` target sounds (they need sim events that do not exist). Observe: `cargo run -p alamo-audio --example audio_hooks -- --md`. Where: `crates/battle-view/src/audio.rs`.
- **Some sounds cannot play because the Remake names files that are not installed:** `EHD_Death_Star_Activate` (`C000_DST0102_ENG.wav`), one sample each of `Unit_Select_Leia` and `Unit_Select_E_Wing`, the Underworld defeat track `Lose_To_Zann_1.MP3`, the empty `SFX_Shield_Absorb_Detonation` (shield hits are silent) and the missing `EnemyUnitDestroyed` advisor event. Observe: the file lists 47 such events. Where: `crates/alamo-audio/tests/known_audio_gaps.txt`.
- **A Death Star battle logs `Sfx UnknownEvent EHD_Death_Star_Begin_Approach`.** Observe: grant `Death_Star_II` to the Empire and fight at Brentaal. Where: `crates/alamo-audio/src/ledger.rs`. (unverified)
- **The ability deactivate sound also plays when a timed ability runs out.** The sim has no deactivation event, so it is detected when the effect time reaches 0. Observe: use `TURBO` and let it expire. Where: `crates/battle-view/src/visuals.rs`, `crates/battle-view/src/audio.rs`.
- **The Remake data itself has broken sound references (for example `Unit_Corrupt_Sabateur`).** Observe: `cargo test -p alamo-audio --test hook_audit`. Where: `crates/alamo-audio/tests/known_audio_gaps.txt`.
- **The death end-sound delay (1.5 s after the start sound) is an estimate.** Observe: listen to a ship death. Where: `crates/battle-view/src/audio.rs`.

## File formats and data

- **Windows and Steam only in practice.** Observe: run on Linux or with a non-Steam install; install detection finds nothing (a Linux Steam path guess exists, untested). Where: `crates/alamo-fs/src/install.rs`.
- **No config file for the game location.** Observe: the game folder can only be overridden with env `EAW_GAME_DIR` or the app's `--game-dir`. Where: `crates/alamo-fs/src/install.rs`.
- **Whole files are read into memory; no streaming or caching layer.** Observe: reading a large MEG entry allocates its full size on every call. Where: `crates/alamo-fs/src/meg.rs`, `vfs.rs`.
- **Patch archive ordering is a guess.** Observe: `Patch.meg`, `Patch2.meg`, `64Patch.meg` are not listed in any `megafiles.xml`; their precedence is assumed. If a file's content surprises you, compare with `megtool vfs-cat`. Where: `crates/alamo-fs/src/vfs.rs`.
- **Ground-unit and story-mission data are only parsed generically.** Observe: such objects appear only in the raw `fields` maps; no typed view exists. Where: `crates/alamo-data/src/objects.rs`.
- **`Depleted_Shield_Regen_Cap` and other Gameconstants are only reachable through the raw map.** Observe: no typed accessor; `CombatTable` exposes the key/value map. Where: `crates/alamo-data/src/combat.rs`.
- **Collision tree (chunk `0x1200`) and chunk `0x1300` extras are ignored.** Observe: `chunk_tree` shows the chunks; the parsed `Model` has no field for them. Where: `crates/alamo-model/src/alo.rs`.
- **Billboard mode 7 and several light fields are partly guessed.** Observe: doc comments marked "Inferred" or "(guess)". Where: `crates/alamo-model/src/alo.rs`.
- **Particle semantics are partly inferred.** Spawn-rate and burst rules, shape block 3, track modes, flags `0x08`, `0x41`, `0x43`, and the `isHeatParticle`, `hasTail` and `noDepthTest` flags are not decoded. Observe: `particle_summary` and `particle_dump` examples; effects can look wrong against the original. Where: `crates/alamo-model/src/particle.rs`.
- **Particle property ids `0x0b` (gravity) and `0x09` (speed) are unverified against the public format table.** Observe: `particle_dump`; the emitter `gravity` field. Where: `crates/alamo-model/src/particle.rs`.
- **`Emitter::oriented` (property `0x08`, `linkToSystem`) is read but nothing uses it.** Observe: grep for `oriented`. Where: `crates/alamo-model/src/particle.rs`.
- **Effect (`.FXO`) sampler states and parameter semantics are not decoded.** Observe: `fx_states` prints `op103[..]` entries undecoded; texture address modes of shield glow textures are assumed to repeat. Where: `crates/alamo-model/examples/fx_states.rs`.
- **Emitter types other than additive are not mapped to blend modes by name.** Observe: `particle_summary` lists type values without a known meaning. Where: `crates/alamo-model/src/particle.rs`.
- **No glTF animation or skin export.** Observe: `alo_to_gltf` writes static meshes only. Where: `crates/alamo-model/examples/alo_to_gltf.rs`.
- **Euler order of asteroid rotations is unverified.** Observe: asteroids in a battle may be tilted differently from the original. Where: `crates/alamo-map/src/lib.rs`.
- **Preview image and the camera chunk are kept opaque.** Observe: `SpaceMap` exposes them as raw bytes. Where: `crates/alamo-map/src/lib.rs`.
- **Many gameplay engine calls are stubs.** Coverage follows what the shipped scripts call; ground-combat calls are ignored. Observe: the log lists each unknown engine call once; run `lua_survey` to see which calls scripts make. Where: `crates/alamo-lua/src/api.rs`, `ai_api.rs`.
- **Cinematic, camera and fog-of-war script calls are no-ops.** Observe: story scripts that fade, pan or reveal the map have no visible effect. Where: `crates/alamo-lua/src/api.rs`.
- **Remake Reforged 5.2 is not a content choice.** `--mod 3372762649` loads about 60 campaigns without warnings, but per-class tech upgrades (`Combat_Bonus_Ability` `*_Lvl0..5_Upgrade`), population dummies, orbital structures with selling and ground companies are not modelled; `INCREMENT_FLAG` rewards are ignored; the data holds both `Empire` and `GalacticEmpire` (and `CriminalUnderworld`); `TaskForce.Get_Goal_Type_Name()` is missing, so 9 `GC_*_CONQUER_UNRESTRICTED_SPACE` scripts fail; the campaign list has no filter.
