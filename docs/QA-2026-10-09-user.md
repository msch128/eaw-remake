## Build rules and data (sim)
- **U1** Buildings are split: space structures (shipyards, Major Construction Yard I/II/III, space station, other
  orbital buildings) belong in the SPACE build menu; ground buildings in the GROUND menu. Today a level 3 shipyard /
  Major Construction Yard is offered in the ground menu.
- **U2** Remove ground buildings that make no sense without ground battles: everything that only produces or supports
  ground units (barracks, factories for ground vehicles, "building 1 for 1700" etc.). Keep economy/defence/bonus
  buildings that affect the galactic game (mines, refineries, academies, shields, income, upkeep, ...).
- **U3** Prerequisites must follow the XML: Major Construction Yard 3 needs 2 needs 1; max-tech buildings need the
  max space station, tech-3 buildings need a tech-3 station, etc. (Required_Special_Structures, Required_Star_Base_Level,
  Required_Ground_Base_Level, Tech_Level, Required_Planets ...). Units of higher tech unlock only after the station is
  upgraded. Not every planet can build everything or upgrade to every level (e.g. only few systems like Kuat build
  super star destroyers, max station level differs per planet).
- **U4** Hero entries for 1 credit show "Requires E_Uplink_Command": the requirement name is a raw object name and the
  hero cost looks wrong. Show the display name of the requirement; check the hero cost/recruit rule in the data.
- **U5** Space station 1 per planet (level 1..max per planet). Space buildings are separate (1..4 per planet, per
  data). Ground building slots: count per planet from the data (Coruscant: 6).
