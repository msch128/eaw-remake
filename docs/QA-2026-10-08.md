### User clarifications (answers to follow-up questions; they supersede the rows above where they differ)
- **Retreat (#23):** the player triggers it; the AI may also retreat when a battle looks bad (damage minimisation). All units
  turn towards their own spawn point and head there to jump to hyperspace. Timer 10 s, maybe 20 s (more depth and a real
  trade-off; make it a named setting). When it expires the jump happens regardless of position: every unit still alive with a
  working engine (if engines are modelled explicitly) escapes. Units spawned during the battle (a Star Destroyer's squadrons,
  a star base's fighters) do not escape on their own (this corrects "follow their carrier" in #23).
- **Escape route:** escaped units fly the direct way to the nearest friendly planet, passing every planet without a new
  battle; if no friendly planet is left, to the nearest neighbour system (first route).
- **Auto-resolve (#22):** must show the same kind of result dialog as a played battle (what was lost, where, what escaped).
- **Duplicates (#27/#28):** the duplicates are in the build lists themselves, mostly the ground/structure menu and also the
  space menu.
- **Planets (#26):** planets are not missing; their names are not displayed at the default zoom (label LOD issue).
- **Left buttons (#25):** all of them; meant are the four small buttons close together at the top-left of the left panel
  (one has a person icon).
- **Formation (#30):** idle ships hold a formation like EaW; at minimum every flier in a squadron stays in formation.
