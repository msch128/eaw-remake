# Roadmap after the user's first full playtest (2026-10-09)

Source: `docs/QA-2026-10-08.md` #21–#30 plus "User clarifications", and the user's screenshots (Brentaal battle,
victory dialog). Honest state: ~66/100. The user played a full GC battle end to end for the first time.
Rule for every item: change only your files, `cargo check --workspace --all-targets`, name your files when you
report, and prove it with a test (unit, smoke or input_test). No item is done without a check that fails before the fix.

## P0: blocks normal play

### 1. Esc / menu does nothing in battle (tactical, space-peace battle_play.rs)
- **Seen:** no pause menu and no save in battle (release 6e4aac9).
- **Code:** `battle_menu` (`crates/space-peace/src/battle_play.rs`) toggles `BattleMenu.open` on Esc and spawns
  `menu::battle_menu_spec()`. The input test passes, so the gap is in the real app.
- **Leads to check, in order:**
  1. Is the system registered and does its run condition hold in the real app, not only under `SP_INPUT_TEST`?
  2. Does the galaxy pause menu also read Esc during a battle, so the two toggle each other off in the same frame?
  3. Are the menu button clicks (Resume, Save, Retreat) routed while the battle screen is up?
  4. Is the menu spawned beneath the tactical HUD (z-order) and so invisible?
- **Also:** the battle menu needs Save, as in the galaxy menu, if snapshot saves can hold a pending tactical battle.
  Otherwise show "Saving is not possible during a battle", which is what EaW does in skirmish.
- **Done when:** a real-input step (Esc in a real windowed run) opens the menu, pauses the clock and Resume
  closes it. A smoke scenario covers it.

### 2. Auto-resolve shows no result (integrator UI, battleloop data)
- **Spec (user):** after auto-resolve, show the same result dialog as after a played battle. It covers:
  - losses per side,
  - where the battle happened,
  - which units escaped.
- **Data:** battleloop makes sure the auto-resolve outcome carries the same fields as `TacticalResult`
  (survivors and losses per group, escaped units). integrator shows it through the existing result dialog.
- **Botajef:** the outcome after losing looked clearly wrong to the user. Reproduce it headless with the campaign
  state if possible, and check owner change and losses.
- **Done when:** a smoke scenario `battle-auto-resolve` asserts that the dialog text has the losses lines.

### 3. Retreat as in EaW (battleloop sim, tactical UI)
- **Today:**
  - `Command::Retreat` exists (`battle-sim/src/sim.rs`). It is refused before `retreat_countdown` (60 s) and
    blocked by interdictors.
  - The AI already retreats (`ai_retreat`).
  - What happens after the order does not follow the user's spec.
- **Spec (user):**
  1. The player (button, plus Retreat in the Esc menu) or the AI orders it.
  2. Every unit stops and turns 180° towards its own spawn side, then heads there.
  3. A jump timer runs: `retreat_jump_seconds`, default 10 (the user said 10 or 20, so make it a named setting).
  4. When it expires, every living unit with at least one living engine hardpoint (`HpKind::Engine`) escapes.
     Ships without engine hardpoints count as having drives.
  5. Units spawned during the battle (a carrier's squadrons, a station's fighters, reinforcement-spawned
     squadrons) do not escape on their own. They are lost unless their carrier escapes. Check this against
     the data for squadrons that belong to a carrier.
- **Keep:** the data-driven "no retreat in the first N seconds" and the interdictor block, if the data defines
  them. Check `Gameconstants` for the retreat values before inventing any.
- **Done when:** battle-sim tests cover:
  - the turn to spawn,
  - the timer,
  - a ship without a living engine not escaping,
  - spawned squadrons staying behind.
  An input_test step presses Retreat and sees the countdown.

### 4. Escape route after a retreat (battleloop, galaxy-sim)
- **Spec (user):** escapees fly straight to the nearest friendly planet, passing other planets without a new
  battle. With no friendly planet left, they go to the nearest neighbour system on the first route.
- **Done when:** a galaxy-sim test covers both cases, and blockades are not applied to the escape move.

## P1: clearly wrong or missing

### 5. The four dead buttons at the top left of the galaxy panel (integrator)
- **Code:** `play_ui.rs` handles only `b_create_*`, `queue*`, `b_space_tab`, `b_planet_tab`, `b_play_pause` and
  `b_fast_forward`. Every other button falls into `_ => {}`.
- **Work:** identify the four small buttons (one has a person icon) by their shell names. Find their EaW function
  (likely filters for the build bar or the units list: heroes, space, ground, structures) and wire them.
- **Done when:** a pointer-test step clicks each one and checks its effect.

### 6. Duplicate entries in the build lists (integrator)
- **Seen:** duplicates mainly in the ground/structure list, also in the space list.
- **Leads:**
  - the same unit offered by several `Build_*` sources (planet, structure, faction),
  - variants with the same display name (`_E`, `_WW`, tech variants).
- **Work:** deduplicate by object type, and if names collide, by display name plus cost.
- **User's proposal:** the left grid filters the build bar by ship class (fighters, corvettes, frigates,
  capitals, structures), as in EaW.
- **Done when:** a unit test over a real planet's build menu finds no duplicate display names.

### 7. Planet names hidden at default zoom (integrator)
- The planets are there; their labels are culled at the start zoom. Tune the label LOD so names of planets near the
  camera show at the default zoom, as in the Remake video (frames v0310–v0520).

### 8. Formations and idle behaviour (battleloop sim, tactical visuals)
- **Seen:** idle ships circle oddly and collide.
- **Spec:** formations as in EaW. At minimum every fighter keeps its squadron formation.
- **Data leads:** squadron member offsets and formation tags in the unit/squadron XML. Check before inventing
  any (e.g. `Squadron_Offsets`, formation entries in the squadron definitions).
- **Work:** idle capitals hold position and facing instead of orbiting. Squadrons fly in their formation and
  keep it while moving.
- **Done when:** a navcheck-style metric gets near zero:
  - idle path length per minute,
  - squadron spread,
  - hull overlaps.

### 9. Battle HUD at Botajef (tactical)
- **Seen:** under attack the left icon column is empty and HUD parts clip off-screen.
- **Work:** reproduce Botajef as defender (station plus fleet), then check the reserve/reinforcement column and
  the HUD bounds at the user's window size (about 1870×1050).

### 10. Hardpoint tooltip (tactical; seen in the user's screenshot)
- **Seen:** "Concussion Missile Launchers - 100 DMG - Debuffs: ... - 65 DMG - 100%".
- **Cause:** the data tooltip already carries a damage figure, and we append ours.
- **Fix:** take the name from `Tooltip_Text`, drop our damage when the text has one (or show only the data text),
  and clamp the tooltip to the screen (it runs off the right edge).

### 11. Attack-line clutter (tactical)
- **Seen:** red lines from every attacking ship to the station stay on.
- **Work:** check the video (battle1s, b0700–b0760). Then show attack lines only for the selection, or for
  about 3 s after the order, like the move lines.

## Release gate (repo-clean)
- Gate builds run in a fresh worktree with `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=target/gate`, never in `target/release`.
- `target/release` (via `build.ps1`) is the user's game exe.
- Before telling the user an exe is ready:
  - full `tools/smoke.ps1` green,
  - `tools/soak.ps1` battle 5 min and galaxy 10 min green,
  - one real-input pass (menu → galaxy → battle → Esc menu → retreat → result).
